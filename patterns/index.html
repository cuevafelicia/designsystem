Accessibility

Page-level accessibility

On top of each component's own rules, the page as a whole follows these.

  • Landmarks and skip link2.4.11.3.1A skip link, one <nav>, one <main> and a <footer>. Each section is labelled by its heading.
  • Heading order1.3.12.4.6One H1 (the project title), an H2 for each section and H3s for cards and journeys. No levels are skipped.
  • Screens have real alt text1.1.1Every app screen describes what it shows. Decorative rings, moons and stickers are hidden from screen readers.
  • Reflow1.4.10Everything fits a 320px wide screen without sideways scrolling, and text can zoom to 200%.
  • Reduced motion2.3.3Tilts, wiggles and hover lifts are turned off with prefers-reduced-motion.
