Component 2.3

Types and colors

The Figma Card set has 6 variants: Type (Insight, Quest) x Color (Teal, Gold, Raspberry). The case study uses Quest cards for the project goals and Insight cards for the research findings.

Type = Quest

Quest 1

Centralize discovery

Users can find a local event without searching across social media, event platforms and websites.

Quest 2

Relevant, not overwhelming

Users narrow events by location, date, type and interests, and feel confident they know what is going on nearby.

Quest 3

Act on it later

Users understand key event details, save events they might attend and come back to them when they are ready.

Type = Insight

It's all in pieces

People found events through Instagram, TikTok, Eventbrite, Meetup, Partiful, venue accounts and friends.

Trust takes work

Participants cross-checked sources to confirm an event was real before deciding.

Screenshots are the save button

Without a good way to keep track of events, people relied on screenshots and memory.

Hover a card to see it tilt. Cards stretch to fill their column, so a row of cards always lines up.

Accessibility

Accessibility guidelines

Cards hold some of the most important content in the case study.

  • Use real headings1.3.12.4.6Each card is an <article> with an <h3> under the section's <h2>, so the heading outline stays in order.
  • Color is decoration1.4.1The teal, gold and raspberry tints only decorate the icon circle. The quest label, title and text carry the meaning.
  • Decorative icons1.1.1The icon circles repeat the title's meaning, so they have aria-hidden="true".
  • Contrast1.4.31.4.11Plum body text on white is 17.9 : 1. The 3px plum border keeps the card edge at well over 3 : 1 against the page.
  • If the card links somewhere2.4.42.1.1Put one real link on the title and stretch it with Bootstrap's .stretched-link. The card then shows the teal focus ring with :focus-within.
  • Reflow and zoom1.4.101.4.4Cards stack to one column at 375px and grow with the text, so 200% zoom never cuts anything off.
  • Reduced motion2.3.3The hover tilt is turned off when reduced motion is on.

Do

  • Keep card text to 1 or 2 short sentences.
  • Use the same type of card across a row.
  • Rotate the three colors in order: teal, gold, raspberry.

Don't

  • Put several links or buttons in one card.
  • Use the tint color to carry meaning on its own.
  • Mix Quest and Insight cards in the same row.
